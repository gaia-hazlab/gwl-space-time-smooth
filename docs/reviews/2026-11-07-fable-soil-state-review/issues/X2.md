## Purpose
Establish whether a cycling filter on MODFLOW 6 can be cold-restarted: UZF carries an internal
wave state (`ntrailwaves`, `nwavesets`) that input files initialise only through `thti`.

## Deliverable
`docs/reviews/2026-11-07-fable-soil-state-review/scripts/x2_uzf_restart.py` and
`results/x2_uzf_restart.json` on the #275 fixture: (1) uninterrupted 60-day run; (2) pause/resume
in one process through modflowapi at every stress-period boundary; (3) cold restart from files at
day 30 (heads from the run, UZF water content from the run's output); heads, UZF water content and
budget compared at the boundaries; MF6 documentation on UZF restart quoted.

## Inputs and conventions
Comparison at existing stress-period boundaries only; off-grid stops are not tested here.

## Acceptance
