## Purpose
Record, with executed numbers, the combined root-zone plus aquifer water ledger of the legacy
store model `src/models/water_budget.py::coupled_water_budget` at `435445f`, separating
manufactured water (capillary credit with no aquifer debit) from the missing valley discharge
(`gw_recess = 0` where HAND <= wt_depth0). This is a diagnostic of the land-surface path that
survives under the MODFLOW partition; it does not ask the replacement to reproduce legacy behaviour.

## Deliverable
`docs/reviews/2026-11-07-fable-soil-state-review/scripts/l1_closed_column.py` writing
`results/l1_closed_column.json`, and `tests/test_ledger_legacy.py`. Cases, all float64 from the
function's float32 outputs, zero P and PET unless stated, `init='wp'`, root depth 1 m, theta
(.10, .28, .45), S_y .30:
1. DTW 1.5 m (table 0.5 m below the root base, inside the 1 m fringe): primary non-overlapping case,
   1 day and 365 days, capillary ON (`cap_max_mm=30`) and OFF (`cap_max_mm=0`).
2. DTW 0.5 m: overlap case (root zone and aquifer claim the same pore volume), ON/OFF.
3. DTW 2.5 m: negative control (beyond root base plus fringe), ON/OFF.
4. HAND = 0, wt_depth0 2 m: (a) rain 5 mm/d, capillary OFF, 365 d; (b) no rain, capillary ON,
   365 d. Head trajectory, day the table reaches the surface, baseflow sum.
5. 365-day fate of manufactured water in case 1 ON: cumulative capillary credit, how much leaves
   as drainage, recharge and baseflow.
6. Storage bound: analytic argument that for P >= 0, PET >= 0 the sequence AET then drainage keeps
   S in [S_wp, S_sat], so the clip at line 251 is a guard; a PRISM-like valid-forcing sweep
   (10 years, 20 cells) counting clip hits; one invalid input (negative P) showing the clip absorbs it.
7. dt 1 d vs 0.25 d drainage totals over 30 days, capillary OFF.
8. Float32 rounding bound: theta resolution times root depth times 1000, reported next to every residual.
9. Context: fraction of `data/processed/baseline_dtw_m.tif` cells with DTW < 3 m (root base + 2 m).

## Inputs and conventions
Residual = dS_root + S_y*dh*1000 + (runoff + interflow + baseflow + AET) - P, in mm per step.
Analytic expectations: ON, 1 day: +0.7392 mm at 0.5 m, +0.2464 mm at 1.5 m, 0 at 2.5 m.
