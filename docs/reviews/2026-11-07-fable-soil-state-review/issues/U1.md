## Purpose
Quantify how much a spatially compound hazard statistic changes when a posterior is sampled from
marginal variances instead of the joint covariance, as a sensitivity demonstration for the
member-set requirement in #187/#188/#193. It is not a calibration claim.

## Deliverable
`docs/reviews/2026-11-07-fable-soil-state-review/scripts/u1_dependence_sensitivity.py` writing
`results/u1_dependence_sensitivity.json`: a 200-cell line of 90 m cells; Matern prior with
sigma 0.5 m, L in {4, 12, 24} km, nu in {0.5, 1.5, 2.5} (`src.models.observability.matern_correlation`
convention); 5 wells with R in {0.1, 0.3} m^2; `blue_update` posterior mean and full C_a (small
case) versus its diagonal; 10 000 draws each way; statistics `P(min DTW over a 20-cell reach < 1 m)`
and `P(any cell in the reach < 1 m)`; binomial standard error; ratio independent/coherent per
(L, nu, R).

## Inputs and conventions
Prior mean DTW 1.5 m; wells at cells 20, 60, 100, 140, 180 observing 1.2 m. Reach = cells 90-109
(1.8 km). Seeds fixed.
