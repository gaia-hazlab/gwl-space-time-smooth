### Problems found

- 'Calibrated or explicitly recalibrated' has no numeric threshold.
- No rule separating calibration data from validation data.

### Physics assessment

Scoring in observation space with native-support operators is correct and important. Three problems.
- PRISM assimilates COOP and SNOTEL stations, so 'evaluate meteorological forcing against PRISM/stations' is partly circular when bias correction is also trained on PRISM. Use separate years and stations independent of PRISM.
- With about 15 wells, stratifying by lead x domain x season x stream x support leaves strata with only a few cases. Minimum sample sizes and bootstrap confidence intervals are needed.
- FuXi daily bins from a 00Z start (fetch_earth2studio.py:154) do not line up with PRISM's 12-12 UTC day, a 12-hour phase error that inflates apparent day-1 error.

## Why this is needed

The repository already implements the correct upscale-then-compare principle, SMAP and reanalysis checks, continuous-well metrics, a staged wet-season/FuXi initialization plan, and static coverage/PIT/CRPS. Those pieces need to become one probabilistic hindcast protocol that scores the cycling nowcast and ensemble forecast by lead time.

Validation must occur in observation space. A 90 m field must be integrated/averaged through the well, probe-depth, coda, pixel, basin, or retrieval operator before it is scored.

## Dependencies

- Depends on nowcast value-added experiment #194 and ensemble forecast #191.
- Builds on #5/#6/#29/#59/#70/#72 and `src/data/stage_mvp_window.py`.
- Uses observation roles/operators from #189 and B/Q/R diagnostics from #192.
- Hazard probability verification consumes outputs from #193.
- Recommended milestone: proposed `v0.7 — Probabilistic nowcast and ensemble forecast`.

## Evaluation protocol

- Wells: screened point/depth support, like-season and continuous-recorder evaluation.
- Soil probes: integrate/compare at instrument depth/support.
- SMAP/NISAR: upscale ensemble members to the native retrieval footprint before scoring.
- GRACE: aggregate storage over native mascon/basin support.
- dv/v: apply the seismic sensitivity/processing operator before comparison.
- Gauges: use basin-integrated flux support and rating-curve uncertainty.
- ERA5-Land/NLDAS: label as model intercomparison, not ground truth.

## Tasks

- [ ] Implement rolling hindcast initializations over the staged wet season with fixed holdouts.
- [ ] Score lead 0 nowcasts and +1…+15 day forecasts separately.
- [ ] Report CRPS/CRPSS, energy score for multivariate state where tractable, interval coverage, PIT/rank histograms, spread-skill, bias, and RMSE.
- [ ] Report Brier score and reliability for state/hazard thresholds.
- [ ] Stratify by lead, domain, season/event, observation stream, and native support.
- [ ] Compare against persistence, climatology, deterministic forecast, and open-loop ensemble baselines.
- [ ] Evaluate meteorological forcing first against PRISM/stations, then state forecasts against independent sensors.
- [ ] Publish machine-readable metrics and reliability graphics with provenance.

## Acceptance criteria

- No coarse observation is downscaled and scored as many independent 90 m observations.
- Probabilistic skill is reported by lead time, not only pooled over the forecast window.
- The ensemble is calibrated or explicitly recalibrated; sharper but under-covering forecasts do not pass.
- DA value added and meteorological forecast value added can be separated.
- Results remain stratified enough to expose sparse-upland and event-regime failures.
- Release gates and baseline definitions are stored in version-controlled configuration.
