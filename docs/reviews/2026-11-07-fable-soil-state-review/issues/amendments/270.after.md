**Amended 2026-11-09 by the soil-state review ladder (#296; record `docs/reviews/2026-11-07-fable-soil-state-review/`).** The published map is static in depth to groundwater; the gate tests a rerun pathway or records that.

**Created from the 2026-10-08 physics and backlog audit (approved by the PI).** Record: `docs/reviews/2026-10-08-physics-backlog-audit/` (PR #229).

## Why
The epic's justification (#139) is unquantified. A probe of Zhu 2017 Model 2 (scratchpad/wf/liq/zhu_sens.py) gives dP = 0.033 for DTW 1 to 5 m, against 0.32 for Vs30 +/-0.35 ln and 0.10 for PGV x1.8. #142's expensive sub-metre DTW work should not start on #141's behalf unless this gate passes.

## Scope
For the pinned model (Zhu 2017 Model 2, or Sanger & Maurer 2025 if weights are obtainable), compute a variance-based (Sobol) decomposition of P/LSE over realistic input pdfs on the Puget lowland: ln Vs30 (sigma from #144), ln PGV (ShakeMap sigma from #143), DTW (pilot seasonal range, plus the static-vs-seasonal-high difference). Acceptance: a table of first-order indices per input; a decision rule recorded in #139: if the DTW share is under 10%, the 'dynamic water table' claim is removed from 07-hazard-integration.qmd and #142's accuracy target is set by #146 only. Coefficients transcribed with a citation and a unit test.
The published Sanger/Geyin/Maurer products are per-cell parameters A and B evaluated with magnitude-scaled PGA (arXiv 2509.10962 v1, Eq. 1 pp. 8-9; user implementation p. 32); depth to groundwater is a static Fan et al. (2013) predictor at ~100 m (Table 1 p. 11) with synthetic deep-groundwater augmentation in training (p. 12); groundwater as a user input is listed as future work (p. 31). The value-of-information gate therefore evaluates a surrogate rerun pathway with DTW as a predictor (validated on observed manifestations, #143), or records that the map is static in DTW and the twin's product is a static susceptibility with seasonal-high DTW as a documented sensitivity.

## Order and dependencies

Blocked by: #143, #144, #260.
