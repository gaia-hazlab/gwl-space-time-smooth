## Why this is critical

The repository already has important probabilistic pieces: calibrated marginal coverage diagnostics, forcing-method ensembles, native-support operators, processing-ensemble dv/v covariance, a correct OU treatment for lagged observations, and a Matérn prior with hydrographic region masking. Those pieces currently live in separate objects, while the operational forecast and snapshot BLUE still exchange deterministic fields and marginal variances.

A fully probabilistic twin needs one contract that preserves dependence across hydrologic states, parameters, forcing, process error, observations, and later mechanical memory. Independently sampling GWL, soil moisture, and parameters can violate the water budget and destroy physically important covariance.

## Dependencies and relationship to existing work

- Depends on #52 for one unambiguous definition of the soil-moisture product.
- Incorporates the corrected Stage-3 uncertainty from #186.
- Can be prototyped against the currently calibrated daily water-budget skeleton, but operational release depends on the storage-account review in #171/#172 and specific-yield separation in #137.
- Builds on the existing native-support observation operators and the OU/Matérn work associated with #161/#163.
- Provides the interface for #162, #28/#29/#32, #71, #121/#122/#134, and hazard handoffs #127/#139.
- Coordinate with #64/#65 and the existing GAIA Zarr/STAC provenance conventions.

## Required state contract

Primary state should be storage based, for example:

`x = [S_root, S_vadose_or_transit, S_groundwater, SWE, S_interflow, bias states]`

The exact storage partition is finalized by #171/#172. Head, DTW, theta, saturation, recharge, and pore-pressure quantities should be derived consistently rather than updated as independent copies.

The schema must distinguish:

- state/initial-condition uncertainty;
- parameter uncertainty;
- meteorological forcing uncertainty;
- process/model-discrepancy uncertainty;
- observation and representativeness error;
- structural/method uncertainty;
- mechanical damage/disturbance uncertainty;
- native versus delivered resolution/support.

## Tasks

- [ ] Add a canonical xarray/Zarr schema with an `ensemble_member` dimension.
- [ ] Define required coordinates, units, masks, hydrogeologic/basin IDs, timestamps, datum age, spatial/depth support, and provenance.
- [ ] Define parameter and forcing member tables and correlation/member-pairing rules.
- [ ] Define observation records, operators, R metadata, and assimilation/validation designation.
- [ ] Define deterministic summaries derived from members: mean, median, sigma, quantiles, and exceedance probability.
- [ ] Define a DA-increment ledger separate from physical fluxes.
- [ ] Add schema validation and round-trip tests.

## Acceptance criteria

- A synthetic coupled ensemble round-trips through Zarr without losing member identity or metadata.
- Soil moisture and GWL are not independently resampled from marginal sigma fields.
- All state variables and uncertainty components declare units and native support.
- Member-level water storage and DA increments can be audited.
- The same object initializes the nowcast, forecast, and downstream hazard handoff.
- Existing deterministic products remain derivable for backward compatibility.

**Added criterion (2026-07-21 Opus strategy review, [`docs/reviews/opus-digital-twin-evolution-review-2026-07-21.md`](../docs/reviews/opus-digital-twin-evolution-review-2026-07-21.md)):** design this contract to be Earth2Studio-shaped *from the start*, because it is the single artifact that decides whether interop is a cheap constraint or an expensive retrofit.

- [ ] The `ensemble_member` dimension and its companions (`lead_time`, `variable`, and the spatial coordinates) follow **Earth2Studio conventions** (coordinate names, ordering, and dims), so the model-structure/assimilation adapter (new Earth2Studio compatibility issue) is a thin wrapper over this contract rather than a schema translation on top of it.

**Acceptance addition:** the canonical object round-trips through an Earth2Studio-style `CoordSystem`/`OrderedDict` coords contract without renaming or reordering dimensions. Rationale: interop is cheapest as a constraint on the contract, most expensive as an adapter over a bespoke schema.
