## Why
The milestone is named for Sanger & Maurer 2025 (a geospatial-AI model, arXiv 2509.10965) while #141 describes a Zhu 2017 GLM. These differ in inputs, availability of weights and output meaning. An agent will otherwise pick one arbitrarily.

## Scope
Human decides: Zhu 2017 Model 2 (+ Rashidian & Baise 2020 caps), USGS ground-failure implementation, or Sanger & Maurer 2025 (confirm weights/licence). Record: inputs and their native datasets (which Vs30, which wtd model the coefficients were fitted on), output meaning (point probability vs areal extent LSE), applicability cutoffs. Label needs-human-decision; #141 is blocked on it.
The published Sanger/Geyin/Maurer products are per-cell parameters A and B evaluated with magnitude-scaled PGA (arXiv 2509.10962 v1, Eq. 1 pp. 8-9; user implementation p. 32); depth to groundwater is a static Fan et al. (2013) predictor at ~100 m (Table 1 p. 11) with synthetic deep-groundwater augmentation in training (p. 12); groundwater as a user input is listed as future work (p. 31). A dynamic groundwater pathway requires a surrogate rerun with DTW as a predictor and validation on observed manifestations (#143); until then the twin's liquefaction product is a static susceptibility.
