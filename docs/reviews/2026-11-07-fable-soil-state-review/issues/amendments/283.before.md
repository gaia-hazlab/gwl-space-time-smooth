Part of the MODFLOW 6 groundwater core. Plan and design record: `docs/reviews/2026-10-18-modflow-groundwater-core/plan.md` (#273); decision issue #265. Epic: #274.

## Purpose
Choose the MODFLOW cell size and layer count from evidence, not habit. The ledger stays at 90 m;
the MF6 grid is k x 90 m (k = 1, 2, 3) nested in it.

## Deliverable
`notebooks/mf6_convergence.py` and a record under `docs/reviews/`: steady-state (and a 1-year
transient if affordable) at k = 1, 2, 3 and at the framework layering vs one extra split of the
top unit. A recommendation table for scale-out.

## Inputs and conventions
Median ridge-to-channel distance computed from the existing flow-routing layers over the pilot
and over the mountain basins. Observation errors from the #189 sigma table, or documented
placeholders if not yet available.

## Acceptance
- For each k: change in head at calibration wells, in simulated baseflow and in SFR gain/loss
  per reach relative to k = 1, compared with the observation error; cells across the median
  ridge-to-channel distance and across the valley-floor width (at least three of each).
- Model bottom: bottom at the bedrock top vs 300 m deeper, compared on deep-well heads and
  baseflow (glacial sediments reach several hundred metres in southwestern King County).
- A recommended k for lowland and for mountain models, with the criterion it meets.
- Runtime per member-year at each k, and the projected cost of an N = 50 cycling filter over the
  pilot and over the full domain. If the domain cost is unaffordable, the PR says so and proposes
  the fallback (watershed models filtered one at a time, or a larger k).
