**Amended 2026-11-09 by the soil-state review ladder (#296; record `docs/reviews/2026-11-07-fable-soil-state-review/`).** Products before the fix listed; export regenerated; replication attached.

**Created from the 2026-10-08 physics and backlog audit (approved by the PI).** Record: `docs/reviews/2026-10-08-physics-backlog-audit/` (PR #229).

## Why
interpolate_residuals.py:203-207 sets dtw_final = baseline_dtw + cr_cube + residual_cube. cr_cube is 'wte_anomaly_m' (:123) and residual_cube is kriged (obs wte_anomaly − cr) (:174); both are elevation anomalies, positive upward (interpolate_anomalies.py:114). So the DTW time variation is sign-inverted, and wte_final = dem − dtw_final inherits the error. The pipeline is live (Makefile:185, pixi.toml:97) and feeds the seasonal-high field used by landlab_export. The bug is recorded only in a #186 science-panel comment (twin-hydrogeologist, 2026-08-20), with no issue.

## Scope
Fix interpolate_residuals.py:203-207 so dtw_final = baseline_dtw − (cr_cube + residual_cube), using hydro_state.dtw_anomaly_from_head_anomaly so the convention lives in one place. Acceptance: a synthetic climate-response zarr with wte_anomaly_m ≡ +1 m and zero residual gives dtw_final == baseline_dtw − 1 and wte_final == dem − baseline_dtw + 1 (test). Grep every other producer that combines baseline_dtw with any wte_* anomaly, and assert the same convention. Add a note to docs/limitations.md listing products generated before the fix.
Products generated before the fix are listed in `docs/limitations.md` with dates; the seasonal-high export and STAC items are regenerated; decision #311 covers withdrawal or relabelling. A 2x2 synthetic replication of the defect is in `docs/reviews/2026-11-07-fable-soil-state-review/results/`.
