## Why
#131 and #87 need storm-lag validation, and #137 needs held-out wells. The repo only has discrete NWIS field measurements (param 72019) aggregated to site-month medians (download_nwis_domain.py:6-15), so no storm-scale lag can be measured.

Calibration targets keep resurfacing as validation: AMP_OBS 1.06 m and the April peak in calibrate_fluxes.py are reused in #6/#100; #29 proposes calibrating to SMAP; #28 anchors to the same SNOTEL stations it validates on (audit S2).
