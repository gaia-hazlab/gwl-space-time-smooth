Part of the MODFLOW 6 groundwater core. Plan and design record: `docs/reviews/2026-10-18-modflow-groundwater-core/plan.md` (#273); decision issue #265. Epic: #274.

## Purpose
Under the #265 partition the land-surface model (LSM: snow, canopy, root zone at 90 m) hands
gravity drainage below the root base to MODFLOW UZF, and MODFLOW hands seepage at its top back
to the root zone. The exchange must move exactly the same water volume both ways across two
nested grids.

## Deliverable
`src/models/mf6_coupling.py`:
- `aggregate(field_90m, k)` and `disaggregate(field_mf6, k, weights)` for a DIS grid of k x 90 m
  snapped to `DOMAIN`; volumes, not depths, are conserved.
- `ExchangeLedger`: per day, LSM drainage out, UZF infiltration in, MF6 seepage out, LSM seepage
  in, rejected infiltration routed to SFR, each in m^3 per MF6 cell.
- Depth to water inside the root zone: the root zone saturates from its base up, with saturated
  thickness s = W_fc+ / (theta_sat - theta_fc) (W_fc+ = root-zone water above field capacity), so
  D = d_root - s while the MF6 head is at or above the root base (the MF6 top).
- Upward flux from a table just below the root base (capillary rise, riparian ET) through UZF
  groundwater ET with an extinction depth below the root base; it is debited from MF6 and
  credited to LSM evapotranspiration in the ledger, never created.
- A daily driver `step(lsm_state, mf6, forcing, day)` that runs the LSM day, sets UZF
  infiltration through modflowapi (or a FINF time series when running uncoupled), advances MF6
  one stress period, reads seepage, and returns it to the LSM. A file-based one-way mode (LSM to
  FINF only) is kept for calibration runs.

## Inputs and conventions
Disaggregation weights for seepage: proportional to the 90 m cells' saturated fraction within
the MF6 cell, documented. Units m^3 and days in the ledger, mm in the LSM.

## Acceptance
- Aggregate then disaggregate of a random field preserves the total to float64 epsilon x cells.
- On the #275 fixture coupled to a two-cell-thick LSM stub, the exchange ledger closes every
  day to the same tolerance, and switching coupling off reproduces the uncoupled run bit for bit.
- A zero-forcing test shows no water created by the exchange.
- With the head held just below the root base, groundwater ET draws down MF6 storage by exactly
  the volume credited to LSM ET.
