**Amended 2026-11-09 by the soil-state review ladder (#296; record `docs/reviews/2026-11-07-fable-soil-state-review/`).** Observable, units, quadrature and a finite-difference forward check precede any normalisation decision.

**Created from the 2026-10-08 physics and backlog audit (approved by the PI).** Record: `docs/reviews/2026-10-08-physics-backlog-audit/` (PR #229).

## Why
The probe (wf/memory/k.py) gives per-band ΣG = 0.46/-0.16/0.44/0.66, with 17-58% of |G| negative, for the repo's pnw_velocity_profile(400) at the DEFAULT_BANDS centers. forward_banded_dvv's docstring (dvv.py:433-435) assumes area-normalized kernels. A uniform +1% ΔVs/Vs predicts -0.16% at 0.71 Hz. Every #198 forward prediction and every depth split depends on this.

## Scope
Determine whether band_sensitivity_matrix returns phase-velocity, group-velocity or coda-intensity kernels, and why lobes go negative (mode osculation at the 50 m soft-layer interface? Vp/ρ coupling?). Determine first what `band_sensitivity_matrix` returns (phase- or group-velocity sensitivity, units, depth node spacing and quadrature weights), then run a finite-difference forward check (uniform +1 % Vs perturbation of `pnw_velocity_profile` -> band response through the forward, or through disba) against G @ m; only then decide whether and how to normalise. Negative lobes and sum(G) != 1 are not defects by themselves. AC: (1) a test that a uniform ε gives band dv/v = ε within 5% for each band, or a documented ΣG_b used consistently in forward and inverse; (2) a figure of G(z) per band; (3) the decision recorded in the #198 operator contract. Read-only use of codameter; report upstream if it is a codameter bug.
