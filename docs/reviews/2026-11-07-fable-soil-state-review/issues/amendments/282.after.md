**Amended 2026-11-09 by the soil-state review ladder (#296; record `docs/reviews/2026-11-07-fable-soil-state-review/`).** Zero-forcing test with the land-surface capillary path off and on; whole-system ledger over the 1-year run.

Part of the MODFLOW 6 groundwater core. Plan and design record: `docs/reviews/2026-10-18-modflow-groundwater-core/plan.md` (#273); decision issue #265. Epic: #274.

## Purpose
Advance the pilot day by day over the hindcast window with the land-surface model supplying
drainage, so that recharge timing (snowmelt, the vadose delay, storms) emerges from the physics.

## Deliverable
Extend #281 to a transient run: daily stress periods on the PRISM 12-12 UTC day, adaptive
sub-steps, UZF on the top active layer receiving LSM drainage through #280, a steady first
period, then a warm-up that repeats the first water year until the per-year head drift is below
1% of the observed seasonal amplitude at calibration wells. Hindcast WY2001 to the latest
complete water year. Outputs: daily heads per layer, UZF water content at OBS depths, SFR flows,
budget, D at 90 m via the 90 m DEM.

## Inputs and conventions
Forcing from #70; snowpack carried per #234. Production-length runs may exceed an agent session:
the agent delivers the configuration, a 1-year run on the pilot, and the run script; the full
hindcast is launched on Hyak and recorded.

## Acceptance
- 1-year pilot run converges; budget per period within threshold; warm-up rule met and recorded.
- Zero-forcing test: heads relax toward the boundary-controlled steady state, never rise; run with the land-surface capillary path OFF and ON (#297 cases) so a rise from manufactured water cannot hide behind the sink.
- Whole-system ledger (#232 b: LSM stores + MF6 budget + exchange + DA increments) closes over the 1-year run within the #275 closure threshold.
- Reported, not gated: lag between LSM drainage peaks and water-table peaks at recorder wells;
  water-table peak month vs observed; seasonal amplitude by HSU.

## Hard blockers
Blocked by: #280, #281, #70, #234.

## Advisory follow-ups
#130 (perched layer) and #283 both reuse this run.
