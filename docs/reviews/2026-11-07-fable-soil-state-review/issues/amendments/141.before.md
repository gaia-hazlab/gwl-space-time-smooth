## Revised scope — approved by the PI, 2026-10-08

**In plain words:** Build only after the model is pinned and the value-of-information gate (#270) shows the water table matters.

**Audit verdict:** rewrite

### Specification

Pin 'Zhu et al. 2017 Model 2 with Rashidian & Baise 2020 caps; coefficients transcribed with a table citation and unit test against the paper's worked values'. Inputs: ln Vs30 with sigma from #144, ShakeMap PGV with sigma_lnPGV from #143, and DTW from a product that does NOT use Vs30 as a predictor (or quantify the collinearity). Monte Carlo over all three. Acceptance: (a) a probability map plus LSE with sigma; (b) a variance decomposition of P into Vs30, PGV and DTW shares, with the pre-registered statement that if the DTW share is under 10% the 'dynamic' claim is dropped from the docs; (c) a hit-rate/ROC against the observed 2001 Nisqually liquefaction sites using the 2001 ShakeMap, stating whether Nisqually is in Zhu's training set; (d) agreement with WA DNR/Palmer reported as a contingency table, labelled a sanity check. Add BLOCKED_BY 141:[143,144].

### Problems found

- The model and coefficients are not pinned
- The acceptance criteria are trivially satisfiable and contain no event validation
- The required time-varying DTW series does not exist (landlab_export.py:333-335)
- Blockers #143 (PGV) and #144 (Vs30 sigma) are not in BLOCKED_BY; the queue runs #141 in batch 25, right after them in batch 24, likely before they merge
- The probability semantics (Zhu probability vs areal extent LSE; PGV < 3 cm/s and Vs30 > 620 m/s cutoffs) are unstated

### Physics assessment

A published logistic screen is the right MVP, but four physics problems need fixing first. (1) The acceptance test 'dynamic DTW changes the map vs static' passes trivially for any nonzero change; under Zhu 2017 the change is at most about 0.03 in P for a 4 m swing, well inside the Vs30-driven sigma_P of about 0.3. (2) ShakeMap PGV already contains a site term computed with the USGS Vs30, and Zhu 2017 was fitted that way. Pairing ShakeMap PGV with the twin's SVM Vs30 applies two inconsistent site models; either use ShakeMap's own Vs30 grid in the GLM or regenerate the ground motion with the SVM Vs30. (3) The DTW product the twin would supply is regressed on Vs30 itself (baseline_regression.py:70; fetch_gaia.py:374-377), so entering both double-counts Vs30. (4) Zhu wtd was fitted on Fan 2013 mean water-table depth; a P10 seasonal high is outside the calibration domain unless refitted or bounded. Benchmarking against WA DNR/Palmer compares an event-conditioned probability with a static geologic susceptibility class (roadmap-review:89); it can serve as a sanity check, not validation.
