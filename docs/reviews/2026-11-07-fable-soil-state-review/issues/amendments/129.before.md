### Physics assessment

Scoring against an inventory is the right validation, and POD/FAR/AUC is appropriate. LandslideProbability is a steady-state, recharge-driven Monte Carlo model; it does not ingest the twin's transient head (followup review lines 60-67). Feeding it the exported recharge_mm is physically inconsistent: that flux excludes interflow (water_budget.py:248-249), while LandLab's R is recharge to the perched layer, so R is underestimated by about (1 - f_lat), roughly 2x at tanB=0.5. 'Timing AND location for both fall shallow and spring deep-seated' is not achievable: most WA DNR inventory polygons are undated, and deep-seated features are outside infinite-slope validity. A slope-only baseline is mandatory, because event slides sit at median 27.9 degrees vs 18.5 degrees for the box (#224), so slope alone gives a high AUC.
