## Defects to fix before use
- Specific storage is added to the capacity but not to θ(h) (`richards.py:21,36-46`). Saturated cells then never converge under a free-drainage bottom (head drifts ~3.5 mm per iteration). Include S_s·h·θ/θ_s in θ(h).
- The time step starts at its minimum and only shrinks on failure; larger steps converge faster in saturated cells.
- No ET, snowmelt input, lateral sink or spin-up.
