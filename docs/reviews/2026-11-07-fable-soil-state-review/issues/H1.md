## Purpose
Give the regional twin a Richards reference it can run today for the option A versus B
root-zone/MODFLOW column test (gaia-hazlab/gwl-space-time-smooth#302): a one-dimensional column
mode of the pinned kernel with the two boundary kinds the 3D kernel lacks.

## Deliverable
`src/hydrostate/column.py` (or an equivalent documented entry point): a 1-D column built on
`flow.RichardsModel` with (a) a free-drainage bottom (unit gradient), (b) a seepage-face top that
switches between flux and zero-pressure head when the surface saturates, (c) a sealed mode.
Two records under `docs/technical/`: the sealed-column conservation control (table at 0.5 m and
1.5 m, no flux anywhere, 30 days) and a matched-configuration capillary-rise record (root zone
1 m; theta_r .10, theta_s .45, alpha converted from the twin's 1/kPa through
`alpha_head = alpha_pressure * rho_w * g / 1000`; initial integrated water from the hydrostatic
profile; table 0.2 m below the root base; zero PET) giving the 30-day upward flux into the top 1 m.

## Inputs and conventions
Same residual, increment and budget checks as `verification.py`; alpha in 1/m; SI units; the
column is synthetic and says so in its metadata.

## Acceptance
- Sealed column: water change <= solver tolerance (1e-10 m^3 s^-1 x duration), no clipping.
- Free-drainage column reaches the unit-gradient steady state within the stated tolerance.
- Capillary-rise record reports flux, cumulative volume and the retention parameters used.
- Existing tests and `hydrostate verify` unchanged.
