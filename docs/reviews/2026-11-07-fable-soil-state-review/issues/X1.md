## Purpose
Decide the root-zone/MODFLOW partition (#265 option A versus B) by experiment on one column
against a Richards reference, before #280 implements the exchange.

## Deliverable
`docs/reviews/2026-11-07-fable-soil-state-review/scripts/x1_option_ab_column.py` and
`results/x1_option_ab_column.json`. One 90 m column, root depth 1 m, soils (theta_wp .10,
theta_fc .28, theta_sat .45), van Genuchten parameters converted once
(`alpha_head = alpha_pressure * rho_w * g / 1000`), water table initially 1.5 m and, in a second
set, 0.8 m. Forcing: (i) zero P and PET, 30 d; (ii) zero P, PET 3 mm/d, 30 d; (iii) 20 mm/d for
10 d then dry 20 d; (iv) 60 mm/d for 3 d. Three representations: R = Richards reference
(hydrostate column gaia-hazlab/hydrostate#1 or #267); A = MF6 top at land surface, UZF owns the unsaturated column;
B = LSM bucket over MF6 with the top at the root base, exchange as #280 specifies. A and B are
MF6 columns on the #275 toolchain.

## Inputs and conventions
Daily output; closure tolerance from the #275 fixture; D reported against land surface.

## Acceptance
- Water-table crossing of the root base under (iii): D continuous across the MF6 top;
  |D_B - D_R| <= 0.1 m at every daily output.
- Storage ownership: LSM S + UZF water + MF6 storage equals the Richards column integral to
  closure tolerance at every output; no pore volume counted twice.
- Pressure continuity: Brooks-Corey psi from UZF water content at the root base agrees in sign with
  R always; magnitude difference reported with the retention-curve mismatch bound.
- Rejected infiltration and seepage under (iv): volumes within 10 % of R, timing within one day;
  B returns seepage to the LSM, not to a sink.
- ET allocation under (ii): AET_LSM + ET_UZF + GW-ET <= PET to 1e-6 mm; residual PET, not full
  PET, reaches UZF/GW-ET.
- Upward exchange under (i) with the table 0.2 m below the root base: root-zone water rises in R
  (capillary rise, an internal transfer). B passes only if an explicit MF6 -> LSM transfer
  reproduces the 30-day volume within 25 % of R, or the omission is declared with its bound.
- Update the TRACKER row X1 with outcome and evidence link.

## Advisory follow-ups
Decision #307 reads this record; #280 adopts it as its acceptance test. UZF represents 1-D
gravity-driven kinematic-wave flow (USGS TM 6-A19), Brooks-Corey K(theta), rejected infiltration,
unsaturated and groundwater ET with an extinction depth, and groundwater discharge to land surface;
it does not represent capillary rise, upward unsaturated flow, lateral unsaturated flow or hysteresis.
