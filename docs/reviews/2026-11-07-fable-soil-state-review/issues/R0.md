## Inputs and conventions
One deliverable per issue; `Refs`, never `Closes`; tolerances from the numerics; only
indispensable hard blockers; dependencies in issue bodies above the first `<details>`;
`needs-human-decision` holds decision issues until a human answers. No issue is closed and no hold
is released by agents.
