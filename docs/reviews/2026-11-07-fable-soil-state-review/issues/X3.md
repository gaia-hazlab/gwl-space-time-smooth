## Purpose

reconciled analysis update against naive clip-and-book on the single-column fixture.

## Deliverable
`docs/reviews/2026-11-07-fable-soil-state-review/scripts/x3_constrained_update.py` and
`results/x3_constrained_update.json`: synthetic cycles on the #289 single-column fixture with
designed violations (analysis table above the MF6 top, root zone outside its bounds, dry cell);
(a) naive update then clip and book; (b) bounded or transformed update, reconciliation through
MF6's storage function and the LSM bounds before the next forecast, clip rule failing the cycle
above a stated fraction of the increment.

## Inputs and conventions
