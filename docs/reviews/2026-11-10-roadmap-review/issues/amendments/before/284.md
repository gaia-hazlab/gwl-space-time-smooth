Part of the MODFLOW 6 groundwater core. Plan and design record: `docs/reviews/2026-10-18-modflow-groundwater-core/plan.md` (#273); decision issue #265. Epic: #274.

## Purpose
Estimate the static parameters with an ensemble method that keeps their uncertainty, using only
calibration-role data.

## Deliverable
`src/models/mf6_pest.py`: a pyEMU PstFrom setup for the pilot: parameters by HSU zone plus pilot
points for log K and S_y; S_s, UZF vertical K and saturated water content, SFR and GHB
conductance multipliers. Prior ensemble from cited per-unit ranges. Observation groups:
calibration-role well heads, seasonal amplitude at recorders, baseflow at the pilot gauge
computed from simulated SFR flow with the same filter used on the observations. Weights from the
#189 sigma table (or documented placeholders). Only well-years whose #235 role is "calibration"
enter; filter-assimilation and held-out well-years are excluded mechanically. Discrete heads are
the raw timestamped measurements, not site-month medians. Per-well offsets (representativeness)
are estimated here, never in the filter.

## Inputs and conventions
Runs on the #275 fixture in CI (small ensemble, one iteration) and on the pilot on Hyak
(#285).

## Acceptance
- Fixture run completes in CI time and writes the posterior parameter ensemble.
- A test proves no well-year outside the calibration role appears in the control file.
- First-order (Schur complement) identifiability and data worth per parameter and per data type
  from pyEMU, reported.
- Wells-blind control: the same setup calibrated on the gauge alone, then scored on the pilot
  wells; reported next to the full calibration.
- Prior predictive check on the pilot (no update): the observed heads fall inside the prior
  ensemble range at a stated fraction of wells; if not, the prior is too narrow and the PR says so.
