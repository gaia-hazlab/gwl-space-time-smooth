### Problems found

- No conservation acceptance (sum of exported volumes = basin budget outflow).
- Per-cell sigma is redundant once members are carried, and invites the marginal misuse the issue warns against.

### Physics assessment

Preserving member identity is correct: independent marginal sampling destroys spatial correlation, which controls basin-integrated tails. The variance of a sum of N correlated cells scales roughly as N^2 rho sigma^2 vs N sigma^2. That regression test is true by construction, so it checks code, not physics. Missing physics: (a) flux ownership. Once #133/#174 deliver interflow downslope, only the interflow that reaches a channel cell may be exported, or water is double-counted (roadmap review R7). (b) Interval integration: the budget is per-step mm (water_budget.py:283), so the export must state the accumulation period and convert to m3/s per cell (mm x 8100 m2 / dt). (c) Baseflow from the HAND recession (:271-281) is groundwater discharge to the nearest drainage; it must map to the reach that drains that cell, not to the cell itself.
