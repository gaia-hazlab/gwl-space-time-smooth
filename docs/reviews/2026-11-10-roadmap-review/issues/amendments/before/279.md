Part of the MODFLOW 6 groundwater core. Plan and design record: `docs/reviews/2026-10-18-modflow-groundwater-core/plan.md` (#273); decision issue #265. Epic: #274.

## Purpose
Pumping is a sink the store model never had. In a lowland pilot it may be comparable to recharge
in places; omitting it silently biases K and recharge in calibration. Septic and irrigation return
flow is the opposite term and is bounded here too.

## Deliverable
`src/data/fetch_withdrawals.py` and `data/processed/withdrawals_domain.parquet`: well location,
screened unit if known, and withdrawal rate (m^3/day) with its basis (metered, permitted, or
estimated from population served); septic and irrigation return flow per cell (m/day) from
county on-site septic records and irrigated area, as a recharge addition. If no usable data
exist for the pilot area, a short memo bounding each omitted term as a fraction of mean recharge
over the pilot domain.

## Inputs and conventions
WA Ecology water-right and withdrawal records and WA DOH public water system sources; document
endpoints and dates. Permitted quantities are an upper bound, not a rate; label them so.

## Acceptance
- Table with basis labels; total by basis for the pilot candidates named in #269.
- If omitted: the bound with its arithmetic, reviewable in the PR.
