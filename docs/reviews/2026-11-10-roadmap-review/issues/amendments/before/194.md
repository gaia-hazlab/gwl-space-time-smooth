- Holdouts must be pre-registered before #192 tuning; that ordering is stated here but enforced nowhere.

The design is right: paired open-loop vs DA runs with identical forcing and parameter ensembles, fully withheld stations, proper scores (CRPS, PIT, spread-error), and an explicit warning that a smaller σ is not success. This is the correct empirical gate (September audit S2).

## Why this is needed

The existing observability maps calculate theoretical resolution and posterior marginal variance, and #5 calibrates static uncertainty. A cycling nowcast needs empirical evidence that DA reduces forecast error and uncertainty where observations genuinely constrain the state.

A smaller posterior sigma is not sufficient: an overconfident filter can shrink spread while becoming less accurate. The test must compare open-loop forecasts and analyses against observations excluded from assimilation.

## Dependencies

- Depends on cycling DA #188, operational observation records/holdouts #189, and calibrated B/Q/R #192.
- Builds on #5 (coverage/PIT/CRPS), #6 (continuous recorders), #29 (native support), and existing observability/resolution metrics.
- Uses the domain stratification from #1/#3 and should coordinate with event hindcast #59.
- Forecast-by-lead verification is handled by the separate dynamic probabilistic validation issue.

## Tasks

- [ ] Pre-register station, time-block, and domain holdouts before assimilation.
- [ ] Run paired open-loop and DA nowcasts with identical forcing/parameter ensembles.
- [ ] Report variance reduction `1 - Var(x_a)/Var(x_f)` at observation footprints and withheld locations.
- [ ] Report CRPS, RMSE, bias, interval coverage, PIT/rank histograms, and spread-error relationship.
- [ ] Report degrees of freedom for signal/observation influence and effective sample size.
- [ ] Stratify by state, sensor stream, hydrogeologic domain, distance/support, season, and datum age.
- [ ] Check whether changes survive withholding entire stations, not only individual observations.
- [ ] Detect and flag posterior collapse, biased innovations, and negative value added.

## Acceptance criteria

- DA improves CRPS over open loop on fully withheld sensors for at least the declared core domains.
- Reported posterior contraction occurs primarily in the observation-constrained subspace; global contraction is not required.
- Nominal interval coverage remains calibrated after DA.
- Assimilated observations are never reused as independent validation.
- Results include failures/negative value added rather than masking them in a pooled score.
- The experiment is reproducible from fixed fixtures/seeds and machine-readable metrics.
