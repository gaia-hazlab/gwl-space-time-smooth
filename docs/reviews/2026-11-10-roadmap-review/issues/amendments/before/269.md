**Rescoped 2026-10-18 for the MODFLOW 6 groundwater core.** Plan and design record: `docs/reviews/2026-10-18-modflow-groundwater-core/plan.md` (#273); epic #274.

## Purpose
Choose the first MODFLOW domain. It must contain wells: the six mountain gauge basins contain
zero NWIS wells (counted 2026-10-18, plan section 2); only Newaukum Creek (12108500, 21 wells,
11 with at least 12 months) and Clarks Creek (12102078, 16 wells) have any.

## Deliverable
A short decision record naming: the domain polygon; its boundaries; the held-out years and
wells (written into the #235 manifest); and the benchmark model, if a published USGS model
covers the area.

## Recommendation (plan section 5)
A domain on the Enumclaw plateau enclosing the whole Newaukum Creek basin, bounded by the Green
River and White River as SFR boundaries with gauged inflows (both regulated: Howard Hanson Dam,
Mud Mountain Dam and the Buckley diversion), and extending into the foothills through a bedrock
unit or a mountain-front flux. The Osceola lahar cap makes most plateau wells semiconfined, a
real test of layer-aware operators. Alternative: Clarks Creek, spring-fed from the Puyallup
valley wall.

## Benchmark
USGS SIR 2024-5026 (Long et al. 2024, doi:10.3133/sir20245026v2), MODFLOW-NWT, 500 ft, 13 layers,
King and Pierce counties, model archive doi:10.5066/P9LU1PMQ. #295 reports whether its footprint
covers the pilot; if it does, it is the benchmark and starting framework. No USGS model specific to
Newaukum Creek or the Enumclaw plateau was found (Crossref search of USGS DOIs, 2026-10-18).

## Decisive check before deciding
Overlay the 12108500 NLDI basin on the DNR bedrock contact (#21) and report the bedrock fraction.
