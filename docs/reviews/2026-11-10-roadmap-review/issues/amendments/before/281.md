Part of the MODFLOW 6 groundwater core. Plan and design record: `docs/reviews/2026-10-18-modflow-groundwater-core/plan.md` (#273); decision issue #265. Epic: #274.

## Purpose
The first real MODFLOW model of the twin: the pilot groundwater domain chosen in #269, built
reproducibly from repo data, run to steady state on long-term mean drainage.

## Deliverable
`src/models/mf6_build.py` and `configs/mf6_pilot.yaml`: build DIS (k x 90 m snapped to
`DOMAIN`, top at the root base per #265), NPF and STO from the #276 framework with per-HSU
prior values (cited ranges), IC, the Newton formulation (dry cells on steep ground), GHB (#10, if the pilot touches the shoreline), SFR (#278),
DRN at the model top for seepage, WEL (#279 or a declared zero), OBS at calibration-role
wells (#235 manifest). Regulated rivers (Green below Howard Hanson Dam, White below Mud Mountain
Dam and the Buckley diversion) enter SFR as specified inflows from their upstream gauges. Mountain
front: overlay the gauge basin on the DNR bedrock contact (#21); if part of the basin lies on
bedrock, extend the domain into a bedrock HSU with K decreasing with depth, otherwise apply a
mountain-front flux from upland LSM drainage along the contact. Steady-state run on mean annual LSM drainage from the existing climatology.
A run record (`runs/mf6_pilot_ss/record.json`) with inputs, hashes and the budget.

## Inputs and conventions
Heads m NAVD88; D = z_s - h_wt reported against land surface (`src/models/hydro_state.py`).
Prior parameters are not tuned in this issue.

## Acceptance
- Converges; budget discrepancy within the closure-justified threshold from #275.
- Reported, not gated: head residuals at calibration wells (by HSU), number and map of dry cells
  and of cells with head above the model top, SFR gaining/losing reach map, simulated baseflow at
  the pilot gauge vs the observed long-term baseflow.
- Mountain-front sensitivity: simulated baseflow at the pilot gauge under no-flow vs the chosen
  mountain-front treatment, reported.
- Rebuilding from the config gives identical input files (hash check).
