Part of the MODFLOW 6 groundwater core. Plan and design record: `docs/reviews/2026-10-18-modflow-groundwater-core/plan.md` (#273); decision issue #265. Epic: #274.

## Purpose
Soil moisture is observed at supports the 90 m root-zone store does not share: probes at
5/20/50 cm, SMAP at 9-36 km for 0-5 cm and the root zone, NISAR at 200 m for the surface.
Each needs its own operator before it can be assimilated or used for validation.

## Deliverable
In `src/models/observability.py` (or a new `src/models/obs_soil_moisture.py`): a probe depth
operator on the LSM within-store profile; a SMAP operator (area-weighted mean of 90 m surface or
root-zone theta over the footprint, with a declared bias treatment: anomaly or CDF matching, chosen
and justified); a NISAR operator (90 m to 200 m aggregation). Each returns the prediction and its
representativeness variance, and declares its observation sigma with a citation (SMAP mission
ubRMSE target; probe and NISAR from instrument or validation papers).

## Inputs and conventions
Theta definition fixed by #52 (which theta is the product). Units m^3/m^3.

## Acceptance
- Probe operator recovers a known synthetic profile at the three depths.
- SMAP operator on a synthetic field equals the analytic footprint mean; partial-coverage cells
  (ocean, outside domain) are handled by a declared rule.
- No operator maps soil moisture onto head or the reverse.
