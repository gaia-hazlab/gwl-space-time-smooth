Part of the MODFLOW 6 groundwater core. Plan and design record: `docs/reviews/2026-10-18-modflow-groundwater-core/plan.md` (#273); decision issue #265. Epic: #274.

## Purpose
The six mountain gauge basins contain zero NWIS wells (count in the plan, section 2). Their
groundwater states can only be constrained by baseflow and recession at gauges, dv/v, GNSS
loading and soil moisture. Recession constrains drainable storage and diffusivity, not K and S_y
separately. The product here is basin storage with an uncertainty; cell heads stay labelled
unconstrained unless this issue shows otherwise.

## Deliverable
Regional PEST++ IES over #287 (compute job, human-launched), plus a synthetic identifiability
test on one mountain basin: recover a known K and S_y field from synthetic gauge, dv/v and GNSS
data alone, with and without each sensor.

## Acceptance
- Posterior vs prior variance of basin storage and of cell heads per mountain basin, with and
  without dv/v and GNSS, from pyEMU Schur data worth and from the synthetic OSSE (true K departing
  from the prior), with degrees of freedom for signal per sensor
  (`observability.degrees_of_freedom_for_signal`).
- A plain statement of which quantities remain prior-dominated.
