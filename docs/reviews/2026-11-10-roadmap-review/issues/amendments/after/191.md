## Why this is needed

The checked-out implementation already has a daily coupled water-budget/snow forecast, a FuXi adapter that supplies precipitation and temperature, explicit guards against zero-placeholder precipitation, and cross-forcing soil-moisture spread. `ForecastForcing` and `SoilStateForecast` still represent one deterministic trajectory, so posterior state covariance and meteorological member dependence cannot be propagated by lead time.

A fully probabilistic forecast must start from the nowcast posterior ensemble, not from a deterministic best estimate plus independent marginal sigma fields.

## Dependencies

- Depends on #187 for the ensemble contract and #188 for posterior initial conditions.
- Builds on #71/#73, the existing FuXi precipitation+temperature adapter, #56/#58, PRISM hindcasts #70, and FuXi execution/bias correction #136.
- Extend #32's forcing/method spread into member-level state propagation.
- Release depends on daily-step and storage-account correctness in #89/#171/#172/#137.
- Uses #155 for reproducible member streams.
- Recommended milestone: proposed `v0.7 — Probabilistic nowcast and ensemble forecast`.

## Tasks

- [ ] Add an `ensemble_member` dimension to forcing, initial state, parameter, and forecast outputs.
- [ ] Initialize members directly from the #188 posterior ensemble.
- [ ] Preserve precipitation-temperature dependence and rain/snow/PET consistency within each atmospheric member.
- [ ] Support calibrated FuXi ensembles first; retain an interface for later multi-model ensembles.
- [ ] Sample spatially correlated parameter fields and process discrepancy without breaking member identity.
- [ ] Propagate complete coupled members through snow, liquid input, soil storage, recharge, groundwater, and dv/v/Vs diagnostics.
- [ ] Separate initial-condition, meteorological, parameter, process, and structural spread in metadata/diagnostics.
- [ ] Write member fields plus mean, median, sigma, quantiles, and threshold probabilities.
- [ ] Keep the deterministic forecast as the one-member limiting case.

## Acceptance criteria

- A forecast initialized from a posterior ensemble preserves state covariance at lead zero.
- Spread evolves with lead time and does not remain a copied static sigma layer.
- Precipitation and temperature members are never independently shuffled.
- Every member satisfies the model's storage accounting and bounds, with any numerical residual reported.
- One-member output matches the existing deterministic forecast within tolerance.
- Output can drive #64/#65 and downstream hazards without reconstructing independent marginals.
- The forecast accepts and returns the complete state of #187; the one-member limit equals the deterministic forecast bit for bit; segmented runs equal uninterrupted runs at day boundaries (#298).
- Posterior initial conditions come from #306 (the pilot slices of #188).

## Order and dependencies
Blocked by: #187, #306, #333.
