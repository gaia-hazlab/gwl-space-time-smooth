### Physics assessment

The candidate set is physically mismatched. FlowAccumulator is a steady flow-accumulation operator (no storage, celerity or attenuation), so it gives no hydrograph timing. OverlandFlow is a 2-D local-inertial shallow-water solver (de Almeida 2012) meant for hillslope/floodplain sheet flow at metre-to-decametre grids, too costly and inappropriate for a basin channel network. HAND-based inundation converts reach discharge to stage and extent via synthetic rating curves; it does not route. A channel-routing scheme (Muskingum-Cunge, kinematic/diffusive wave via t-route or RAPID, mizuRoute) on NHDPlus HR reaches is what produces 'a routed hydrograph'. The 'no downscaling that manufactures information' rule is correct.
