### Physics assessment

The framing is correct: the twin exports antecedent source terms, and routing/inundation is a separate model on a reach network (consistent with the closed #55/#39 and 07b-resolution-requirements.qmd:17-23). Three problems. (1) The candidate list is physically wrong: FlowAccumulator has no travel time or attenuation, and HAND is an inundation-mapping step (it needs reach discharge plus synthetic rating curves), not routing. (2) A daily source term cannot resolve peaks in basins whose time of concentration is under a day; most Cascade headwater basins under about 200 km2 respond within hours. (3) Snowmelt and rain-on-snow (#177 m1) dominate PNW flood antecedents and are missing from the scope.

## Why this epic exists

Flood is one of the twin's named target hazards, alongside landslide (#127) and liquefaction (#139),
but it is the only one without an epic. Milestone "Hazard: flood / inundation handoff" existed empty until this epic. Flood is
**structurally different** from the other two: landslide and liquefaction consume the soil state *at the
90 m grid cell where the state lives*; flood consumes the state as **runoff / interflow / baseflow source
terms routed along a channel/reach network** — a different spatial object entirely. This is the
"native support out" half of the project's symmetric scale principle (see
[`docs/reviews/opus-digital-twin-evolution-review-2026-07-21.md`](../docs/reviews/opus-digital-twin-evolution-review-2026-07-21.md) §3): native support in (observations), native support out (hazards).

## Scope

- **Exports (what leaves the twin):** runoff + interflow + baseflow source terms with **per-cell
  uncertainty**, at 90 m, as the **same joint ensemble members** #193 passes to the other hazards — so
  cross-cell spatial correlation in the source term survives routing. This epic is a **#193 consumer**,
  not a standalone pathway.
- **Routing (what a downstream model consumes):** reach/link discharge. Candidate components: LandLab
  `OverlandFlow` / `FlowAccumulator`, or an external HAND-based inundation model.
- **Validation:** routed discharge against USGS gauge hydrographs — reuse the D7 BFI / Q–P calibration
  already landed for the gauged basins (#98) — and, where available, observed inundation extent.

## Explicit non-claim

The twin does **not** produce flood extent on its own 90 m grid. It produces antecedent-state source
terms; routing and inundation are a separate model. This matches the disclaimer already established for
#39 (Earth2Studio flood-scope) and #55 (antecedent-state reframe; routing is LandLab's job).
