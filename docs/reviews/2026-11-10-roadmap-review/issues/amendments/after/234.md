**Created from the 2026-10-08 physics and backlog audit (approved by the PI).** Record: `docs/reviews/2026-10-08-physics-backlog-audit/` (PR #229).

## Why
liquid_input resets SWE to 0 on every call (forecast.py:123), so mid-winter forecasts have no pack. Rain passes through the pack instantly (forecast.py:130), and warm atmospheric-river melt is under-predicted. Rain-on-snow is the PNW extreme-antecedent flood mechanism.

## Scope
Carry SWE (and pack liquid water) in the state and in the forecast initial condition. Add liquid holding capacity (about 5-10% of SWE) with refreezing, and a rain-on-snow melt enhancement (advective heat plus a wind/turbulent DDF term). Acceptance: leave-stations-out SNOTEL melt-out and RoS event tests (e.g. Dec-2025 AR); conservation including the snow store.
One snow owner: `forecast.liquid_input` and `soil_moisture.snowmelt_liquid_input` are consolidated per decision #309; SWE is carried across calls and restartable; a segmented run (10 + 10 days) equals the uninterrupted 20-day run bit for bit at day boundaries (the #298 `xfail` test flips).

## Order and dependencies
Blocked by: #263.
