Part of the MODFLOW 6 groundwater core. Plan and design record: `docs/reviews/2026-10-18-modflow-groundwater-core/plan.md` (#273); decision issue #265. Epic: #274.

## Purpose
Extend from the pilot to the lowland groundwater basins and the eight gauged basins, one GWF
model per watershed with its own parameter zones, run in one MF6 simulation so that aquifers
crossing surface divides are not cut by artificial no-flow edges.

## Deliverable
Generalize `src/models/mf6_build.py` to a list of watershed domains; GWF-GWF exchanges along
shared lowland boundaries; no-flow at upland bedrock divides (tested); the k chosen in #283;
a parallel run recipe for Hyak, including compiling the extended MODFLOW 6 build (PETSc + MPI;
the conda-forge package is serial). A steady-state and 1-year transient run of the full set.

## Acceptance
- Exchange fluxes balance across every shared boundary (sum to zero within the budget threshold).
- A divide test: for one upland divide, compare no-flow vs a joined exchange and report the
  difference in baseflow on both sides.
- Budget per model and for the whole simulation.
