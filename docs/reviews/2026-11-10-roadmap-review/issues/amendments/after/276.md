## Purpose
MODFLOW layers follow hydrostratigraphic units (HSUs), so every model needs unit top and bottom
surfaces. The twin has a surface lithology class raster (#21) but no subsurface framework.

## Deliverable
`src/features/hydrostratigraphy.py` and `data/processed/hsu_framework_90m.zarr` on
`src/config/domain.DOMAIN`: for each HSU, top and bottom elevation (m NAVD88), thickness, a
presence mask, and a one-sigma elevation uncertainty, with source and digitizing provenance per
cell. A short doc section in `docs/twin/` naming the units and their sources.

## Inputs and conventions
- Lowland units (top down): Holocene alluvium / Vashon recessional outwash; Vashon till; Vashon
  advance outwash; pre-Vashon fine-grained; older coarse; bedrock surface. Uplands: colluvium,
  weathered bedrock, fractured bedrock, with thickness from SOLUS depth-to-restrictive-layer and
  the depth-to-bedrock sources already in the repo; say which.
- Sources, in order of preference: the 13-layer framework of USGS SIR 2024-5026 (Welch et al. 2024,
  doi:10.3133/sir20245026v1) inside its footprint, exported by #295; the regional Puget Sound
  framework (Vaccaro, Hansen & Jones 1998, USGS PP 1424-D, doi:10.3133/pp1424D) and the Puyallup
  framework (Welch et al. 2015, doi:10.3133/sir20155068) outside it; DNR geology for unit
  outcrops (#21). Cite each with a DOI verified by `pixi run check-dois-metadata`; if a DOI cannot
  be verified, do not add it to `docs/references.bib` and say so in the PR.
- Surfaces honour land surface (no unit above z_s) and are monotone (top ≥ bottom).
- Nearest-neighbour or documented interpolation only; never smooth across a mapped fault or
  outcrop contact without saying so.

## Acceptance
- Monotonicity and land-surface checks pass for every cell (test on a fixture tile).
- Coverage table: fraction of the lowland and upland domain where each unit comes from a
  published surface vs a fallback.
- Comparison at wells: where a well report or NWIS hole depth is available, report how often the
  framework's unit at the screen depth agrees with the logged unit (no tuning to that number).
