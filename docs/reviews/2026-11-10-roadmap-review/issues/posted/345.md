# Change-of-support operators with their variance, and a support stamp on every product

**Created 2026-11-11 from the roadmap review (record `docs/reviews/2026-11-10-roadmap-review/`, epic #319).**

## Purpose
MODFLOW cells may be k x 90 m under a 90 m ledger (#283): one 270 m head copied to nine cells is
one cell's information shown nine times. Storm-time landslides need 1 to 10 m. #191 outputs
per-cell sigma and threshold probabilities with no support field. No issue owns either step.
`src/evaluation/uncertainty_stack.py:191-199` adds dependent sigmas in quadrature with an
undeclared 5.0 m constant, which #32 forbids.

## Deliverable
Conditional-simulation operators for 270 to 90 m and 90 to 10 m that add the unresolved variance,
`support_m` and a resolution width on every #335 product, and removal of the quadrature stack
from any delivered path.

## Acceptance
- On Matern truths with the #299 parameters, 90 % coverage at 10 m support within +-0.05.
- A test refuses to write any sigma or probability without a support attribute.
- A k = 3 product at 90 m reports the same information content as at 270 m (degrees of freedom
  for signal unchanged by the copy).
