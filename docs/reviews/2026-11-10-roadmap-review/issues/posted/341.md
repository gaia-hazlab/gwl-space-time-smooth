# Event-mode pressure-head column for storm-time slope stability (claim or non-claim)

**Created 2026-11-11 from the roadmap review (record `docs/reviews/2026-11-10-roadmap-review/`, epic #319).**

## Purpose
The regional model is a daily root-zone bucket "with no profile" over a kinematic-wave vadose zone
without matric potential. Transient psi(z,t) over 0 to 3 m at hourly steps, the quantity that
triggers shallow slides, has no owner; the 10 m step (#133) is steady and one-way; #128 notes h_w
from the regional table "is near zero on most hillslopes". Perched water also straddles two owners
wherever colluvium is deeper than the root depth (#130 in MODFLOW, #174 in the land surface).

## Decision requested
Is storm-time shallow-landslide triggering a claim of this system? If yes, this issue is P0 for the
landslide milestone and blocks #224. If no, #127 states antecedent-state susceptibility only.

## Deliverable (if a claim)
A 1-D Richards column (the #267 solver or hydrostate column mode) run in event mode at selected
hollow columns, initialised from the member state, forced hourly (#332), returning psi(z,t).

## Acceptance
- Peak pressure head at 1 m within 0.1 m of the reference solution on the #302 forcing set, or
  storm-time FS is labelled "unsupported" in every export.
- A column with 2 m of colluvium over till and a 1 m root depth: pressure is continuous across the
  MODFLOW top and the ledger closes; the test names which component owns the perched water.
- Segmented run equals continuous run (state carried, #298 rule).
- Water moved by initialising the column from the member is booked.
