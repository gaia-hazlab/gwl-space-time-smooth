# Unsaturated strength, a suction-stress closure with its own acceptance

**Created 2026-11-11 from the roadmap review (record `docs/reviews/2026-11-10-roadmap-review/`, epic #319).**

## Purpose
#128's plain-words line says "with suction"; its acceptance tests `closure={'hydrostatic','slope_parallel'}`
only, and `src/models/hydro_state.py:221-223` returns suction as zero by contract. No issue has an
acceptance line for unsaturated strength. Loss of suction is the usual trigger of shallow failures
in colluvium before any positive pressure develops.

## Deliverable
A third closure `suction_stress` in `src/models/slope_stability.py` (Lu and Likos form,
sigma_s = -S_e psi), fed by effective saturation and matric head from the retention curve.

## Acceptance
- At h_w = 0: FS - FS_dry = sigma_s tan(phi') / (gamma z sin(beta) cos(beta)) to 1e-6.
- At S_e = 1 the closure equals the saturated closure to 1e-6.
- Sensitivity table: change in FS for a 0.05 change in saturation at 1 m depth on 30 and 40 degree
  slopes, for two retention parameter sets, so the consumer sees what daily bucket saturation can
  and cannot resolve.
