## #168 Coda footprint operator bias into R; 3-D (not separable 2-D) kernel
- verdict: **rewrite** (verifier upheld); agent_ready: False; missing blockers: [198]
- physics: (i) The edge bias is real and still present: pair_kernel renormalises the in-grid part to 1 (dvv_sensitivity.py:134-137) and only documents it in the docstring. The fix is to compute the kernel on a padded domain, keep the out-of-domain mass f_out, and add roughly f_out²·σ_m² to R. Do not renormalise. (ii) The separability claim is overstated. For surface-wave-dominated coda, a lateral 2-D kernel times a Rayleigh depth sensitivity is the standard approximation (Obermann et al. 2013/2016). It fails at later lapse times, when the coda partitions toward body waves. The physically right target is Obermann's combined kernel α(t)K_2D·K_z + (1-α(t))K_3D, not a full 3-D kernel by itself. (iii) The constants are inconsistent. D=8 km²/s and v=1.5 km/s give a transport mean free path l* = 2D/v ≈ 10.7 km and a mean free time of about 7 s. At t_lapse = 10 s (≈1.4 mean free times) the diffusion approximation is not valid; radiative-transfer kernels (Paasschens 1997) are needed. Also, dvv.py:232 sweeps coda windows from 5 to 50 s, but the operator uses a single 10 s kernel. The diffusion radius √(4Dt) runs from 18 km at 10 s to 40 km at 50 s, so the operator does not match the datum. rainier3d cannot provide near-surface Vs(z) here, because it flags its top 300 m as unresolved (rainier leverage review row 6).
- problems:
  - 'Use a 3-D kernel' is not specified enough to implement, and it is not the right physics target for early coda.
  - It overlaps #198's depth-kernel operator.
  - No acceptance.
- proposed edit: Rewrite with three acceptance items. (1) Padded-domain kernel with f_out reported per pair and its variance added to R; a test shows a pair straddling the boundary gives an unbiased innovation on a synthetic. (2) A kernel for each lapse window actually used, consistent with DEFAULT_CODA_WINDOWS. (3) A kernel ensemble over D, l* and the partition coefficient α within stated ranges, with the spread propagated into R. Use radiative-transfer kernels when t_lapse < ~3 l*/v, or state the regime of validity. Coordinate depth kernels with #198.
