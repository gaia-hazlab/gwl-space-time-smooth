## #50 P1: Per-hydrostratigraphic-unit k_sat (confined vs unconfined: Sy vs S, drained vs undrained)

## #121 Coseismic damage + log-linear healing as a carried dv/v state
- verdict: **rewrite** (verifier upheld); agent_ready: False; missing blockers: [198, 124]

The body is the surface-disturbance text that belongs to #122, so no coseismic specification exists anywhere. A correct specification is as follows. The damage state D(x,z,t) enters ΔVs(z,t) in the #198 operator, concentrated in the top tens of metres. The jump size is a saturating function of PGV or dynamic strain from ShakeMap; observed drops are ~0.1-1% at 1-2 Hz for regional M6-7 shaking and much larger very near the surface, so this is an order-of-magnitude prior. Healing should follow the Snieder et al. 2017 relaxation spectrum, dv/v ∝ ∫_{τmin}^{τmax} e^{-t/τ} dτ/τ, which is log-linear for τmin ≪ t ≪ τmax. To keep the state Markov, represent it as a Prony series of 4-6 carried exponentials with log-spaced τ. The single exponential in #190 is the wrong law (roadmap-review R5). Healing must not feed liquefaction triggering (#145).
